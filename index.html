About Ademola
Founder · Djedi Skool of S.O.U.L.
Ademola Michael Okubena
CEO · Rubicon Wellness LLC · Djedi Master
"I am here only to be truly helpful — to bring all that I have learned, mastered, and know concerning health and wellness to the whole world, sharing these gifts I have been given by the Source of all things."
The vision
Ademola Michael Okubena is the CEO and Founder of Rubicon Wellness LLC in Colorado — a company rooted in the belief that the human body-mind deserves simple, natural solutions to thrive. RUBICON represents a turning point in humanity's relationship with health: moving beyond dependence on conventional pharmaceutical therapies and returning to natural, plant-based solutions with little to no side effects.
The journey
With over 25 years of intimate knowledge in the natural products industry, Ademola has helped supply natural food ingredients to the Dietary Supplements market worldwide — developing the family brand Sorghum Superfood JOBELYN, sold to millions across Africa for decades. He helped conduct human and clinical studies in Africa, Europe and the USA, validating what was known historically: that Sorghum's powerful polyphenol antioxidants rapidly reverse cellular inflammation at the root of many degenerative conditions.

His need for healing and deep fascination with miraculous transformation took him on a journey across three continents — a path of self-discovery that divinely ignited his passion for African mystic arts, metaphysics, esoterism, spirituality, and human psychology. Formative traumatic and near-death experiences became the very fires that forged his calling.
The teaching
Holding a Bachelor of Arts in Business Management and Entrepreneurship from Kingston University London, Ademola now joyfully shares the healing principles of Pure Non-Dualism — integrating mind, body and soul through the wisdom of Kush-Kemet, A Course in Miracles, and Capstone Spiritual Technology. He is a life coach drawing on more than twenty years of lived experience, a strong advocate for Black mental health, and a living embodiment of the teaching he carries.

Off the stage and out of the boardroom you will find him on the tennis court, in quiet meditation, or planning his next adventure abroad — disciplines that sharpen his mind, ground his spirit, and fuel the empathy he brings to every session.
